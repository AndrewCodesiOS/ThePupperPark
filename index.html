How to Play PupperPark

Tap GrassDrop extra Bones or rare Magic Bones yourself.
Tap a PupPet them for hearts, a happy bark, bonus XP, and +5 happiness (once every 20m per dog).
Swipe & PinchDrag left/right to rotate the park; pinch to zoom in or out.
Call PackHold on a tile (1s) or tap Call to gather your pups together.
Decor & ToysPlaced decor grants permanent XP, Magic, and Bone bonuses; toys boost digs & offline yield.
Hourly WeatherWeather shifts every hour with atmospheric effects and perks (tap the weather badge for details).
Pack Growth & LevelsEvery pup's bones dug boost the whole pack's Dig Multiplier (scaled by Pack Growth), and each dog level adds +1% to its own bone yield.
Pack HappinessPetting, Happy Snaps, treats, and toy play easily lift your pack to Happy (50%) and Joyful (70%). Your park starts at 80% Max Happiness — placing Park Decorations smoothly raises the cap toward 100%, unlocking Ecstatic at 90%+ and giving you more cushion to maintain it!
